SOP - POST-MIDTERM WEEKLY SHORT PUT, WITH THE THREE-MODEL / THIN-PREMIUM GATE
Rule E3 ("Unanimous_IV"), as implemented in weekly_signal_tradesheets.xlsx
Source of truth: risk_gate.py, build_baseline.py, weekly_put_variants.py,
                 make_notebook.py            Written 2026-11-17
===============================================================================

0. SCOPE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0.1  Instrument traded      SPY weekly put, 1 contract, multiplier 100.
0.2  Instrument modelled    SPX daily tape (1963 onward) for all features and
                            labels. The models forecast SPX weekly excursions
                            in %; the trade is executed and stopped on SPY.
0.3  Trading window         1 Jan -> 15 Jul of the year AFTER a US midterm
                            election (post-midterm year). Nothing is traded
                            outside it.
0.4  Cadence               One trade per Monday-week, 28 weeks per cycle.
                            Cycles with option data: 2011, 2015, 2019, 2023
                            = 112 weeks. Next window: 1 Jan - 15 Jul 2027.
0.5  Structure              Naked short put. No 3% OTM long wing
                            (BUY_3PCT_OTM_WING = False).

1. WEEK DEFINITION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  A week runs Monday to Friday. The entry session is the FIRST exchange
     session of that week inside the window; the exit session is the LAST.
1.2  A Monday holiday moves entry to Tuesday. The week is never skipped for a
     holiday. Weeks with fewer than 2 sessions are dropped (none occur).
1.3  Weeks in the sample: 88 full 5-session, 22 of 4 sessions, 2 of 3.

2. ENTRY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Time: 09:30 of the entry session. Fill = OPEN of the first option minute
     bar at or after 09:30. If the chosen strike has not printed by 15:00,
     the week is not traded.
2.2  Strike: the listed strike NEAREST the entry spot (= the entry session's
     SPY open). If that strike has no entry bar or no exit bar, step out to the
     next-nearest, up to 8 tries. Realised strike distance from spot in the
     sample: median 0.07%, max 0.34%.
2.3  Expiry: the weekly expiry nearest the week's last SCHEDULED session
     (typically Friday). Time to expiry at entry: 4.27 days (4-session weeks
     are shorter).
2.4  Direction: SELL to open, 1 contract. Premium received = entry_price.
2.5  Entry spot (entry_spot) is fixed at the fill and is the reference for
     every level below. SPY prices must be on the TRADED (unadjusted) scale -
     the dividend-adjusted minute file prints SPY at 96 in 2011 vs 127 traded,
     which selects a penny strike. spot_bars.py rescales it; do not bypass.

3. LEVELS  (percentages of ENTRY SPOT, never of the premium)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  TARGET  = entry_spot x (1 + 1.5995%)      index moves FOR the position
3.2  STOP    = entry_spot x (1 - 1.1306%)      index moves AGAINST it
3.3  Both levels are on the UNDERLYING (SPY). The option premium never carries
     a stop or a target of its own.
3.4  Both levels are live on every bar of the hold, the last session included.

4. MONITORING AND EXIT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  MONITORING GRID = DAILY (1D) BARS. This is the grid the 5,075 baseline was
     produced on and it is the live rule.
       - target touched  : session HIGH >= target
       - stop touched    : session LOW  <= stop
       - a session that touches BOTH is booked as the STOP (the daily bar does
         not say which came first).
4.2  EXIT ON A TOUCH: the option's CLOSE at 16:00 of the SAME SESSION the level
     was touched on. The position is NOT closed at the moment of the touch.
     Expect to sit through the rest of that session - median 3.5 hours.
4.3  EXIT WITH NO TOUCH: the close of the last option bar of the week's last
     session (effectively 15:59 Friday). Booked as TIME_EXIT.
4.4  There is NO intraday stop and no 1-hour trigger. Replacing 4.2 with an
     exit at the close of the touching HOUR costs 1,005 over the 112 weeks
     (stops -794, targets -211): the end-of-day fill is the better rule in this
     sample and is the rule of record.
4.5  P&L = (entry_price - exit_price) x 100 x 1 contract.
4.6  Sample outcome mix: STOP 45, TARGET 36, TIME_EXIT 31 of 112.

5. THE SIGNAL - HOW A WEEK IS JUDGED
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  THREE QUANTILE-REGRESSION MODELS, no logistic regression. Each is fitted
     on POST-MIDTERM WEEKS ONLY, EXPANDING BY CYCLE: the 2011 cycle trains on
     1963-2007, 2015 on 1963-2011, 2019 on 1963-2015, 2023 on 1963-2019. No
     week is ever scored by a model that saw it.

     Model A  "month+rv+path"      (11 features)
              m_Feb m_Mar m_Apr m_May m_Jun m_Jul   (Jan is the base level)
              rv5 rv20                              (annualised realised vol %)
              dist_high20 mom_4w mom_12w
     Model C  "month+rv"           (8 features)
              m_Feb m_Mar m_Apr m_May m_Jun m_Jul rv5 rv20
              -> NOTE: C is a strict SUBSET of A. They are not independent.
     Model B  "lookback, no calendar" (11 features, look-back family: l1_*,
              r4_*, path). Its exact membership is NOT recorded in the repo -
              the script that fitted it is missing and only its cached output
              survives (data/processed/gate_hold_B_lookback_no_month.pkl.gz).
              Re-derive and write it into risk_gate.py before trading it.

5.2  FEATURE TIMING. Every feature must be known at the fill:
       - month dummy uses the week's SCHEDULED last session;
       - realised vol is stamped on the last session BEFORE entry;
       - every look-back feature is shifted so the week sees only weeks that
         CLOSED before it; rolling windows are applied AFTER the shift.

5.3  PROBABILITIES. Fit each model at 19 taus (.02 .04 .06 .08 .10 .13 .16 .20
     .25 .30 .35 .40 .50 .60 .70 .80 .90 .95 .98) on both targets, SORT each
     week's predicted quantiles (to remove quantile crossing), then invert at
     the trade's own levels:
       p_stop   = the tau at which Q_tau(dd_w)  = -1.1306%
       p_target = 1 - the tau at which Q_tau(mfe_w) = +1.5995%
       edge     = p_target - p_stop
     Clamp to the ends of the tau grid; never extrapolate.

5.4  PREMIUM TEST ("thin_pay"). At the fill:
       pay_pct = entry_price / entry_spot x 100
       pay_ref = the expanding MEDIAN of pay_pct over the EARLIER weeks of the
                 SAME cycle, shifted one week, minimum 3 observations
       thin_pay = (pay_pct <= pay_ref)
     Sample: pay_pct median 0.71% of spot; 61 of 112 weeks are thin.

5.5  THE DECISION (rule E3, the Unanimous_IV sheet):
       SKIP the week  <=>  edge_A <= 0 AND edge_B <= 0 AND edge_C <= 0
                           AND thin_pay is TRUE
       otherwise TAKE at full size.
     No fitted threshold, no skip fraction, no quantile cut. The only numbers
     are a zero and a median.
     Sample: 28 weeks skipped, 84 taken.

5.6  SIZING. Binary - 1 contract or nothing. No partial size.

6. WHAT TO RECORD PER WEEK
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
monday, entry_date, exit_date, entry_strike, entry_spot, entry_price,
exit_price, pnl, exit_reason (STOP / TARGET / TIME_EXIT), target_pct, stop_pct,
edge_A, edge_B, edge_C, votes_edge, p_stop, p_target, iv_atm, pay_pct, pay_ref,
thin_pay, decision, size, pnl_gated, pnl_forgone, gate_outcome.
pnl_forgone is signed: + = profit given up, - = loss avoided.

7. HARD CONSTRAINTS - DO NOT CHANGE WITHOUT A RERUN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1  Stop 1.1306% and target 1.5995% are fixed inputs, not tuned here.
7.2  Trigger grid stays 1D (see 4.4).
7.3  Models are refitted ONLY between cycles, on post-midterm weeks only,
     expanding, never on the window being traded.
7.4  Thresholds stay at 0 (edge) and the shifted expanding median (premium).
     Re-introducing a fitted skip fraction invalidates the result.
7.5  SPY prices stay on the traded scale (2.5).

8. KNOWN LIMITS - READ BEFORE SIZING UP
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1  Sample = 4 option cycles, 112 weeks. Baseline (take every week) 5,075.
     E3 gives 5,610, i.e. +535, at 1.28 saved per 1.00 of profit sacrificed
     (1,878 sacrificed against 2,413 avoided).
8.2  That +535 is one cycle: 2015 +964, 2023 +22, 2011 -135, 2019 -316.
8.3  Models A and C are near-duplicates (edge correlation 0.91, Cohen's kappa
     0.82 on the skip vote); effective independent dimensions across the three
     models is ~1.9, not 3. "Unanimous" is closer to two votes than three.
8.4  The premium test does the work: thin-premium alone is -553, the spot
     signal alone is -1,556 to -3,263, and C-alone + thin premium gives +602 -
     more than the three-model unanimity.
8.5  The stop does not cap the daily loss: once the stop level is touched the
     index had already run a median 1.82% (mean 2.05%) below entry on the hour,
     and 16 of 46 touch-weeks closed that session still below the stop.
8.6  The stop level is touched in 46 of 112 weeks (41%) - in 45 of them while
     the position was open. 17 of those touches land in the 09:30-10:30 hour,
     i.e. 5.5 hours of holding before the 16:00 fill.
